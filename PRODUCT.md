# Product

## Register

product

## Users
People running local-first personal AI agents on their own hardware. They value speed, efficiency, privacy, and full control over their computational resources.

## Product Purpose
A local-first personal AI assistant client interface ("Orion AI"). It serves as the hub for running reasoning tasks, scheduled background monitoring, and code execution entirely locally without cloud dependence.

## Brand Personality
"Iron Man J.A.R.V.I.S but more Modern and Sleek." High-tech, elegant, focused, and deeply futuristic but stripped of unnecessary visual clutter. It feels intelligent, responsive, and deeply integrated into the user's workflow.

## Anti-references
- Avoid chaotic, overly busy "cyberpunk" or 90s hacker-movie interfaces. Needs to remain sleek and minimal.
- Avoid typical, generic, flat light-mode SaaS dashboards. It should feel like a premium personal tool.

## Design Principles
- **Sleek Intelligence:** Communicate high capability through minimal, highly-refined visual feedback rather than overwhelming data displays.
- **Form Follows Focus:** Ensure the UI stays out of the way of the core AI interactions, illuminating only what is necessary at any given moment.
- **Living Interface:** Motion and transitions should feel like a breathing, thinking entity ("Orion") rather than a static webpage.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Maintain high legibility and contrast against dark backgrounds. Ensure complex animations (like HUD elements) gracefully degrade for users who prefer reduced motion.
