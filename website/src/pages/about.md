# About Orion

Most "personal" AI assistants send every request to someone else's servers: your questions, your habits and your conversations pass through a cloud you don't control. Local AI models are now good enough that, for a lot of everyday help, that trade-off isn't necessary.

**Orion is built around that idea.** It's an assistant that lives on your own Windows PC. It thinks with a local model, listens and speaks with local voice models, and remembers what matters in a knowledge graph on your disk. It only reaches out to the internet when a task genuinely needs it.

## What makes it different

- **Autonomous.** It doesn't just chat: it opens apps, plays music, changes settings, drafts messages and emails, searches the web and drives your browser. Anything irreversible waits for your approval.
- **Persistent.** It remembers your preferences, notes and past conversations across sessions instead of starting blank every time.
- **Self-developing.** When no existing tool covers a request, it can write a new one, which is tested and only added after you approve it.

## Who builds it

Orion is designed and built by **Tharun**, as an independent open-source project. Every part of it, from the desktop app and voice to the installer and this website, is developed in the open on [GitHub](https://github.com/AstraDev-Labs/Orion-AI).

## Where it's at

Orion is in **alpha**. It works, and it has rough edges. The [roadmap](/roadmap) shows what's next, and the [changelog](/changelog) what's changed. The best way to help is to [try it](/download) and [report what breaks](/contact).
