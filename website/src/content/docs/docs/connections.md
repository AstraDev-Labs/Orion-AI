---
title: Connections
description: Link WhatsApp, email, Telegram and other services to Orion.
---

Open the **Connections** screen (press **C**) to link services. Each card says what the connection is for and what it needs. Keys and tokens you enter are saved only on your PC, in the `.orion` folder in your user profile.

Services marked as online send requests to that service when used. Everything else Orion does stays on your computer.

## Available connections

| Connection | What it enables |
| --- | --- |
| Email | Read your inbox and draft emails |
| WhatsApp | Read and draft WhatsApp messages; away-mode replies |
| Telegram | Messages through your own Telegram bot |
| Discord | Messages through your own Discord bot |
| Slack | Messages in your Slack workspace |
| Web search (Tavily) | Higher quality search results (DuckDuckGo works without a key) |
| Obsidian vault | Use your Obsidian notes as memory |
| Notion, GitHub, Dropbox | Search and work with your content there |
| Spotify, Strava, Oura | Music control, activity and health data |
| Weather | Your default location |
| OpenAI (optional) | Use a cloud model. Personal details are removed before sending |

## WhatsApp

1. In Connections, open **WhatsApp** and start linking.
2. On your phone, open WhatsApp → **Linked devices → Link a device** and scan the QR code shown by Orion.

:::danger[Unofficial client]
Orion's WhatsApp connection uses an unofficial WhatsApp Web client. WhatsApp doesn't endorse it, and using it may break WhatsApp's terms and put your account at risk. Only use it if you accept that risk.
:::

## Disconnecting

Open the connection's card and choose disconnect, or remove its key. For WhatsApp, also remove the device from **Linked devices** on your phone.
