## What Orion can do

- **Apps and media:** open and close apps, play music and videos, control playback.
- **Your PC:** volume, brightness, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, battery and system status, screenshots.
- **Information:** web search, live weather, calculations, time and dates.
- **Memory and notes:** remember facts about you, write notes, search what you've told it.
- **Reminders:** timed reminders and recurring schedules.
- **Files:** find, read and organise files you point it at.
- **Messages and email:** draft messages on connected services.
- **Software:** find and install apps with winget.
- **New tools:** when nothing fits, Orion can propose a new tool for you to approve.

## Approvals

Some actions can't be undone, so Orion asks first:

- **Sending** a message or email.
- **Installing** software. Orion checks the package's signature and scans it with Microsoft Defender, then tells you what it found.
- **Adding** a tool Orion wrote itself.

Orion describes exactly what it's about to do. Reply **yes** to go ahead, or anything else to cancel. Pending approvals are also listed in the **Governance** screen.

Only you can approve. The AI has no way to approve its own actions, and actions that change something only run when your request actually asks for them.

## Away mode

Away mode lets Orion reply to incoming messages while you're busy. It's off until you turn it on ("turn on away mode"). Every automatic reply says it's from your AI assistant. Turn it off with "turn off away mode".

## Things Orion won't do

Shutdown and restart are disabled. The desktop-control tool refuses buttons like Send, Delete or Buy, and won't touch banking or password windows.
