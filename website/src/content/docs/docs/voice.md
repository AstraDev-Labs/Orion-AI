---
title: Talking to Orion
description: Use your voice with Orion, and control when it speaks.
---

Orion's speech recognition (Whisper) and voice (Kokoro) both run on your PC. Your audio is never uploaded.

## Speak to Orion

1. Click **Auditory** in the top bar to turn the microphone on.
2. Speak normally. Orion notices when you stop talking, transcribes what you said and replies.
3. While Orion is speaking it stops listening, so it doesn't hear itself, then listens again.
4. Click **Auditory** again to turn the microphone off.

The first time, Windows asks for microphone permission. Allow it.

## Voice replies

By default Orion speaks every reply, including answers to typed messages. To keep typed conversations silent, open the Auditory panel and switch **Voice replies** off. Replies to spoken questions are always spoken.

## Getting better recognition

- Use a headset or sit close to the microphone, away from fans and speakers.
- Pause briefly before and after a command.
- Close games and other heavy apps. Recognition and the AI model share the same processor.
- Check Windows is using the right microphone: **Settings → System → Sound → Input**.
